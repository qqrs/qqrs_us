    20-30 minute meditations

    Nov 09, 20262 min read

    These are guided meditation recordings that have been played at RC’s Monday Meditation.

    Monday Meditation greatest hits

    • Thich Nhat Hanh: Mindfulness of breathing 20 min

    “Breathing in, I know I am breathing in."
    "Breathing out, I know I am breathing out.”

    • Can You Zen:

      • Guided Breath Meditation (Anapana) 30 min
      • Vipassana Meditation 30 min

    • Rob Burbea: Practising the Jhānas - Counting Within the Breath 30 min (0:38 - 30:00)

    • Shinzen Young: How to structure your practice in life 28 min (6:40 - 34:30)
      noting vipassana: feel, hear, see, do nothing

    • Brightmind:

      • Thoughts & Emotions - Feel In
      • Physical Senses - Hear Out
      • …

    • Michael Taft: Do Nothing Meditation, aka “Dropping the Ball” 31 min

    • Tasshin: Guided meditations for love practice various, most are ~30 min
      mettā and the brahmavihārās

    • Sharon Salzberg: 10-Day Lovingkindness Challenge various, 15 min

    A few shorter ones

    • Pema Chödrön: Tonglen Meditation 12 min

    • Daniel Ingram: How To Awaken From Emotional Turmoil 6 min

    A few others

    • S.N. Goenka:

      • Mini Anapana Meditation 10 min
      • Group Sitting - Dhamma Setu - 2000 32 min
        Hindi dohas and Pali chanting

    • Marvin G. Belzer: UCLA Mindful: Mindful Monday various, 30 min

    • The Rubin Museum: Mindfulness Meditation various, ~20 min

    • Ethan Nichtern: A Meditation on Hope and Fear 30 min
      breath; hope & fear; open awareness

    • Tara Brach: Breath And Awareness 26 min

    • Kaira Jewel Lingo: Fresh, Solid, Calm, Free 28 min

    • David Johnson: TWIM Guided Beginner Meditation 30 Min
      TWIM mettā

    • Ajahn Brahm: Guided Metta Meditation (26 May 2015) 29 min (0:00 - 29:15)
      kitten mettā
